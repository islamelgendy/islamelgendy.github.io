## Research Interests  

My research focuses on **Software Engineering**, particularly **Software Testing and Verification**. My key areas of interest include:  

- **Diversity-Based Testing Techniques** 
- **Search-Based Software Testing**  
- **Mutation Testing**  
- **Automated Test Data Generation**  
- **Test Case Prioritization and Selection**  
- **Test Suite Reduction**   
- **Large Language Models for Software Testing**

I am currently working on projects that apply diversity-based principles to improve **software testing efficiency and effectiveness**.  

## Ongoing Research  

I am currently investigating the use of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** to enhance software testing through diversity-driven approaches, including:

- **Generating Diverse Test Cases Using Large Language Models:** Exploring the effectiveness of LLMs in producing diverse and high-quality test suites to improve fault detection.  
- **Large Language Models as Guides for Diversity-Based Test Prioritization:** Investigating how LLMs can support and guide test case prioritization strategies by incorporating diversity-aware insights to optimize test execution.  

These projects build on my ongoing work in **diversity-based testing**, extending it towards intelligent and adaptive testing methodologies.
